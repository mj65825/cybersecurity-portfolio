# ATCAP Home Healthcare Solutions: Senior Capstone

Team project (M&T Solutions) for the George Mason University IT capstone, completed over two semesters. I was the team lead on a five-person team.

## Client

ATCAP Home Healthcare Solutions, a home healthcare organization in Woodbridge, VA. The goal was to improve their operational efficiency, digital presence, and application workflow.

Live site: https://www.atcaphomehealthcare.com/

## What we delivered

1. A reliable, accessible website to improve ATCAP's online presence and reduce downtime.
2. A structured job application system so interested workers can apply more easily.
3. An improved application review process using a Python script to organize, access, and review applicant information.

The solutions were designed around ease of use, low cost, scalability, and efficiency, giving ATCAP a 24/7 accessible platform and less manual work.

## My role

Team lead: ran conversations with the sponsor, managed scope and team responsibilities, and kept the project moving through pivots and technical challenges.
